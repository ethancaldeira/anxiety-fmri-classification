# Atlases and masks

All files are label images on the MNI152 3 mm grid (61 × 73 × 61, the grid
of the C-PAC `space-MNI152NLin6ASym` 3 mm output and of the ABIDE PCP
derivatives). `src/compute_features.py` loads an atlas by file name
(`--atlases <name>` reads `<name>.nii.gz` from this directory).

## Standard atlases (Chapter 5)

The seven PCP atlases are the ROI atlases that the Preprocessed Connectomes
Project distributes with its ABIDE release
(`s3://fcp-indi/data/Projects/ABIDE_Initiative/Resources/<name>.nii.gz`,
described at <http://preprocessed-connectomes-project.org/abide/Pipelines.html>),
nearest-neighbour resampled onto the 61 × 73 × 61 grid; the labels are
otherwise unchanged. They are redistributed under the ABIDE / INDI terms
(Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike); the original
atlases remain subject to their authors' terms of use.

## Stochastic parcellations (Chapter 6)

| Files | Origin |
|---|---|
| `SP_v2_<scale>_<sample>.nii.gz` | 30 Poisson-disk parcellations at each of 50, 100, 150 and 200 ROIs (120 files), generated by `src/generate_sp_masks.py` from the grey-matter mask below (random seed = sample index). No subject data are involved. |

## Group parcellations (Chapter 6)

| File | Origin |
|---|---|
| `group_gm_low.nii.gz`, `group_gm_mid.nii.gz`, `group_gm_high.nii.gz` | Group-mean normalised-cut parcellations (K = 200; 197, 200 and 199 non-empty labels) of the GSP training subjects in the low, mid and high STAI-T tertiles (cut-offs 32 and 40, training split only), built with [anxiety-fmri-parcellations](https://github.com/ethancaldeira/anxiety-fmri-parcellations). They are group-level derivatives of GSP data, contain no individual-level data, and are shared under the GSP Open Access Data Use Terms. |

## Grey-matter mask

`../masks/grey_matter_mask.nii.gz` (49,786 voxels) is the MNI152 3 mm
grey-matter mask of the Ensemble3DCNN_connectomes repository of Khosla
et al. (2019), <https://github.com/mk2299/Ensemble3DCNN_connectomes>
(`misc/grey_matter_mask.nii.gz`, byte-identical copy). It restricts the
voxel-wise fingerprints to grey matter and is the domain of the stochastic
and custom parcellations.
